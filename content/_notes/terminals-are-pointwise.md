Limits of diagrams in a functor category $[\mathbf{I}, \mathbf{C}]$ are
not necessarily pointwise when the category $\mathbf{C}$ is not
complete. We show that no counterexample exists for terminal objects,
or more generally, for limits of diagrams that take values in the
constant functors.
