Atlantic Category Theory Seminar

The ATCAT Seminar is a seminar on topics in category theory organized by the Atlantic Category Theory Group, running since 1973.

  • Regular time: Tuesdays at 2:30 PM Atlantic time
  • Location: Chase Building, Room 319

Virtual participation is available. Please contact the organizer (Hayato Nasu) to be added to the mailing list and receive the Zoom link.
