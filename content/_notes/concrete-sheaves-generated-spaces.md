convenient categories of spaces, mostly through multiple nLab entries
and references on them. I found the general theory interesting and
beautiful, but results seem scattered. I leave this article incomplete
so that I can update it as I learn more, and it might be useful for
others as well. Feedback, comments, and suggestions are very welcome.
